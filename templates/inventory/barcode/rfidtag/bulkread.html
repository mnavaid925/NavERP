What Happens on Ingest

Each submitted EPC is matched against the tag registry:

Matched Tag's last-seen timestamp is stamped now; reader location is recorded when one was selected.
Unknown EPC Counted in the ingest result — never dropped or failed — so you can spot unregistered tags in the field.

Ingesting a dump does not move stock, create lots, or alter tag lifecycle status. It only refreshes last-seen telemetry.

Warnings

Retired and lost tags are still matched — their last-seen data updates even though they are out of service.

Duplicate EPCs within one dump are collapsed to a single read.
