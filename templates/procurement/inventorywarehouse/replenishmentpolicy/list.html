How a policy is chosen

specificity, then nothing else

When a replenishment run needs the policy for an item at a location, the most specific active policy wins: a policy naming that location beats the item's any-location catch-all, and if neither exists the run falls back to the SCM reorder rule alone. Inactive policies are never resolved — keep one inactive rather than deleting it when an item is phased out, so the history stays readable.

Only Buy raises a requisition. Transfer and Manufacture are recorded and reported on, but a run will not quietly purchase something that should have been moved between warehouses or made in-house.
