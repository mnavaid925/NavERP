A policy is procurement's overlay on an SCM reorder rule. The rule owns when to reorder — the reorder point and the safety stock — and nothing here restates either. This form owns what the rule cannot say: who to buy from, how much to round the quantity to, and what the generated requisition carries.

How the quantity is rounded

the order is the contract

A shortfall becomes a suggested quantity in exactly this order, and the order matters:

  1. Never negative. A shortfall of zero or less is not an order.
  2. Floor at the minimum order quantity.
  3. Round up to the next multiple.
  4. Cap at the maximum order quantity.

Because the cap is applied last, it wins over the multiple: with a multiple of 30 and a cap of 100, a shortfall of 95 comes back as 100, not 120. That is deliberate — a ceiling a caller can exceed is not a ceiling — and it is why a maximum below the minimum is refused rather than left for the two rules to fight over.
