Danger zone

Deleting a policy does not touch the SCM reorder rule, the item or any requisition already raised. What it removes is procurement's glue: this item falls back to its any-location policy, or to no policy at all, and a run then has no vendor, no rounding and no requisition defaults for it. Deactivating is almost always the better move — an inactive policy is never resolved, and the history stays readable.
