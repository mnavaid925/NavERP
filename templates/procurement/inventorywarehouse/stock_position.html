How available is calculated Available = on hand − (sales-order allocations + inventory reservations) − non-sellable stock. It is the same formula the warehouse stock-levels page and the reservation check use, and it is deliberately not floored at zero: a negative figure means more has been promised than is held, which is the thing worth seeing.

How below-point is decided On hand plus whatever the governing replenishment policy counts as inbound, against the reorder point — the run's own trigger, applied here with the same two policy switches the run applies. A policy that turns off Include on-order or Include open requisitions makes this column ignore that supply, deliberately, so an item can read below point while an order for it is already outstanding. The On-order and Open-requisition columns beside it are never gated: they always show what actually exists.
