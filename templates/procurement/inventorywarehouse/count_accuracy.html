What this page does not know {{ attribution_note }}

Net versus absolute: net variance is the sum of counted minus the sum of expected, so a +5 and a −5 cancel; absolute variance adds their sizes instead. Read net for the effect on stock value and absolute for how much counting actually disagreed.
