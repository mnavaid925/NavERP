COMPUTED and READ-ONLY, like its sibling dashboard: no table, no migration, no write. It answers the category-manager's questions — who supplies this category, how concentrated is it, what is the price spread on the same item, and how long is the tail — over records that already exist. Nothing here does arithmetic. Every share, cumulative share, spread and concentration figure arrives already computed from analytics.py; the one comparison this template makes is banding the HHI against the two thresholds printed next to it, which is a legend, not a metric. HHI is the sum of every supplier's squared share, scaled to 10,000. Under 1,500 the category is spread across many suppliers; 1,500–2,500 is moderately concentrated; above 2,500 a small number of suppliers hold most of it, which is leverage for them and risk for you.
