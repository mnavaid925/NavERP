SpendReportSnapshot has no create/edit form by design (the documented CRUD exemption): a snapshot is minted only by the spendreport_snapshot POST, and a hand-typed one would be a figure with no run behind it. It therefore has a detail page, a CSV export and a POST delete — and no form.
