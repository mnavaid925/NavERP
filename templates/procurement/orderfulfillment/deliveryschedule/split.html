{% comment %} Spell out the two things that surprise a buyer the first time they use this page: what is actually divided (the remainder, not the ordered quantity) and where the rounding goes. A multi-line {# #} would leak into the rendered HTML as visible text (L2), so this is a {% templatetag openblock %} comment {% templatetag closeblock %} block. {% endcomment %} What gets divided is the quantity not already committed to instalments — on a line with no schedule rows yet that is the whole ordered quantity. The last instalment absorbs the rounding remainder, so the rows always sum back to the remainder exactly. Need-by dates start at the first date and step forward by the interval. A line that is already fully covered is refused rather than over-committed, and the whole split is written in one transaction — two buyers splitting the same line at once cannot both succeed.
