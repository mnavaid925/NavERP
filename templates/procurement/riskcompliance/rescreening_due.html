How due dates are worked out

no stored flag, no background job

A supplier's due date comes from its most recent Cleared screening: the next-re-screen date if one was set, otherwise a year from the day it was screened. A supplier that has never been cleared is past due by definition and sorts to the top. Pending, escalated and blocked screenings do not count — an undecided check is not a clearance.

Nothing on this page writes anything. It tells you which suppliers to screen; recording the screening is a deliberate act, on the register.
