How this board is worked out

no stored flag, no background job

Each supplier is tracked one series at a time — one provider, one metric — and only the latest reading in each series counts. A series whose refresh date has passed is overdue. One whose reading has aged past the staleness window is stale, whatever its refresh date says, because an old number is an old number. One falling due inside the next thirty days is due soon. Anything comfortably fresh is simply not listed.

A supplier with no observation at all is never monitored rather than overdue: nobody promised a refresh date for it, so it cannot be late for one. What is true is that there is no data, which is staleness taken to its limit — so it sorts to the top of the board regardless. A series against a party that has since lost its supplier role drops off this board entirely; the register still shows it, but nobody is being asked to refresh it.

Nothing on this page writes anything. It tells you which readings to refresh; capturing the observation is a deliberate act, on the register.
