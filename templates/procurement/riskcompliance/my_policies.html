Before you sign

there is no un-sign

Signing records that you have read the policy, on today date, under your own account. It cannot be undone and nobody can do it for you — an administrator who tries is refused by the system, not by convention, because a signature somebody else could apply on your behalf would not be evidence that you read anything.

If you genuinely should not have to sign something on this list, ask an administrator to record an exemption. That is a different thing and it says so on the record: it keeps the fact that you were asked, names who decided you did not have to, and requires them to write down why. It is never counted as a signature.

Each version of a policy is signed separately. Signing version 1.0 says nothing about version 2.0, and when a new version is published you will be asked again — which is the point, because the thing you agreed to read was the text in front of you at the time.
