How the coverage figure is worked out

an exemption is not a signature

The percentage counts signatures against the size of the roster. Exemptions are excluded from it on purpose: an exemption is a decision to stop asking somebody, made by an administrator with their name and reason on the record, and counting it as a signature would let a workspace reach a hundred per cent without a single person having read the policy. If a number of exemptions looks high, that is a fact worth seeing rather than one worth rounding away.

Only the person named on a sign-off can sign it — not an administrator, not a superuser, not anybody acting on their behalf. That is the whole evidentiary value of the ledger. The administrative answer to somebody who genuinely should not have to sign is the exemption, which is a different word with a different meaning and its own audit trail.

Nothing on this page is a control. Signing a policy grants no access and blocks no purchase, and no page or spend anywhere in this system is gated on whether somebody has acknowledged a policy. Approval routing is decided by the approval workflow engine, and that is the only thing that actually enforces anything.
