Who owns what on this page

one policy library, one sign-off ledger

The policies themselves — writing them, versioning them, publishing and archiving them — belong to the policy library. This page owns the other half: the roster of people asked to acknowledge each one, their signatures, the chase, and the exemptions. There is one table behind both, so a policy cannot exist in one place and not the other, and there is no second register competing for the same idea.

Raising attestations is safe to press again at any time. It assigns the policy to everybody it applies to who is not already on the roster, and it creates nothing for the people who are — no deadline is moved, and no signature already on file can be disturbed. That is what makes it the repair button for somebody who joined the department after the policy went out, as well as the button that starts the roster in the first place.

A policy with acknowledgment switched off collects no sign-offs at all, deliberately: the flag is the workspace statement of intent for that policy, and a flag the ledger ignored would be decorative. Turn it on in the library first, then raise the roster here.
