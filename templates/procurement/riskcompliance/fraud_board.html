Likewise the open/high figures in the by-rule table are NOT links. What this board does not cover

cited, never re-detected

Two neighbouring questions look like this one and are answered elsewhere. This module does not re-detect either of them, because two modules disagreeing about the same fact is worse than one module answering it.

Duplicate invoices — the same invoice submitted twice. Detected on the invoice itself, where the amounts, dates and supplier reference actually live, and worked in the invoice module.

Maverick spend — spend that went around the agreed process: no contract, no order, off catalogue, above the contracted price, or split below an approval threshold. That is a question about PROCESS. Everything on this board is a question about INTEGRITY, and a split purchase in particular is a control weakness rather than an accusation the evidence would support.
