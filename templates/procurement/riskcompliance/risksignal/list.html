Why the Band column, and not the Value column

the scales run in opposite directions

RapidRatings FHR runs 1 to 100 where 100 is healthy. Dun & Bradstreet Supplier Evaluation Risk runs 1 to 9 where 9 is dangerous. Sorting a shared score column would put the two safest suppliers in this workspace at opposite ends of the register, so each row carries its own metric and the system converts the raw number to a risk position where 0 is always the safest end. The Band column is the only column comparable across providers.

A metric with no registered scale bands as Not banded rather than as low. Saying that nothing is known is honest; defaulting to a clean band would be a fabricated all-clear. And a band is advisory throughout — it colours a badge, raises an inbox item at worst, and blocks nothing. The only place a vendor is actually blocked is the vendor suspension register.
