Nothing matches these filters. A commitment appears when a purchase order is approved (or later) or a requisition is approved and not yet converted — and disappears again when the document is closed or cancelled.

Clear filters

A requisition is counted once: once it is converted it IS its purchase order, and the register shows the order instead. Draft and cancelled orders were never commitments; closed ones no longer are. Nothing on this page is a stored encumbrance — the accounting module's budget holds the only amounts, and this register only points at documents.
