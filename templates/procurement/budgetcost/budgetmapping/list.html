How a mapping is chosen

specificity, then priority

When the system needs the budget for a department or a project, the most specific active mapping wins: a project mapping beats a department mapping, which beats the workspace default (both dimensions blank). Within the same tier, the lower priority number wins and ties break on the oldest mapping. Inactive mappings are never chosen — keep one inactive rather than deleting it when a budget winds down.
