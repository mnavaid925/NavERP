How search and expiry work here

what the register can and cannot find

Searching sweeps the number, title, description, tags and the text read from the document's currently approved file. Text is read from PDFs that carry a text layer and from plain-text uploads; a scanned image has no text to read, so such a document is findable by what people typed about it rather than by what is inside it. Re-index re-reads files for documents whose stored text is empty.

Expiring soon means the expiry date falls inside the next 30 days; Expired means it has passed; Review due means the review date has arrived. Past retention is a flag for a human to act on — nothing on this page deletes anything on a schedule, and deleting a document never reclaims its stored files from disk.
