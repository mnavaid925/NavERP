What happens next

the file, the revision and the approval

{{ search_note }}

Saving creates the record with no file and no approved version. From its page you upload the first revision, and approving that revision makes it the document's current version, copies its text into what search matches, and moves the document from draft to active. Uploading on its own never changes which version is current — that is what keeps the chain honest.
