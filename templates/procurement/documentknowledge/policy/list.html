What this library does — and what it does not

read this before quoting a number off a policy page

{{ advisory_note }}

A guideline figure on a policy is the number the written rule quotes, so a buyer can read it next to the rule it belongs to. It is documentation. The approval bands that actually decide how many signatures a requisition needs are the routing rules in Approval Workflow Engine (6.3), which resolve department, commodity and amount to a required tier count and are enforced when an approval is recorded.

Publishing a version puts it in force and archives the version it replaces, so the register never shows two published versions of the same rule. Archiving takes a policy out of use without deleting anything — it keeps its text and its place in the version history.

Where a policy is flagged for acknowledgment, that is a marker for Policy Management & Acknowledgment (6.17), which owns assignment and sign-off. Nothing here records who has read a policy.
