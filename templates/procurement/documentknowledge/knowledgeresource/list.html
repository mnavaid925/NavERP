What this library is — and what it is not

read this before treating a stored template as something that runs

{{ library_note }}

Everything here is content people read. Opening a scorecard tells you how bids are meant to be compared; it does not score anything. Opening an RFP template shows you the wording that works; the event suppliers actually answer is built in Sourcing & Tendering (6.5) and its questionnaire in 6.6, and the requisition blueprints that genuinely raise a purchase live in Requisition Management (6.2).

The downloadable file behind a resource is an ordinary document in the repository, so it carries a revision chain, an approval step and searchable text instead of sitting somewhere as an unversioned copy.

The "Used" column counts presses of the Use button on a resource page and nothing else. It does not know who pressed it and it is not evidence that a template was used on any event — the audit trail is what records who did what.
