House rules

  1. Eligibility. Only suppliers invited to an auction may bid, and only while its window is open (published and inside opens/closes).
  2. Opening ceiling. A supplier's FIRST bid must be at or below the auction's start price.
  3. Lowering pace. Every later bid must undercut that supplier's own previous best by at least the minimum decrement — tiny self-improvements are refused.
  4. Real improvement. A bid must also strictly improve the current global best (equal amounts are rejected), so the leader can never be overtaken without progress.
  5. Anti-snipe extension. A bid placed within the extension zone of the close automatically pushes closes_at out by the configured seconds — capped at the maximum number of extensions so an auction always ends.
  6. Append-only log. Bids are never edited or deleted; corrections appear as new, lower bids. Rankings are recomputed from the log and never stored.
  7. Award integrity. An award can only be recorded once, only from a closed auction, and only in favour of the current leading supplier.
