performance against risk, both axes already on the row

Two axes, both already on the row: the composite score above, and the risk index from the supplier's most recent risk assessment dated on or before this period end (a 1-5 mean, where lower is safer). A supplier scoring 70 or better sits in the top half of performance; a risk index of 2.5 or less sits in the safer half. A supplier missing either axis is left Unplaced rather than being guessed into a corner.

Strategic scores 70+, low risk — keep and grow.
Hidden high performer scores 70+, higher risk — performing well on borrowed time.
Development scores under 70, low risk — worth the coaching.
Underperforming scores under 70, higher risk — the improvement-plan conversation.

Rank and percentile are computed across the suppliers shown above and nobody else: the percentile is the share of the scored cohort a supplier is at or above. Change the tier or category filter and both change, because the cohort changed. Suppliers with no scored line are listed last and are given no rank at all — an absence of evidence is not a score of zero.
