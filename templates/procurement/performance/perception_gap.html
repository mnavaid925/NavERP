Reading the gap

gap = their self-rating − our rating

+20 or more a wide gap — they believe they are performing far better than we think.
+10 to +20 a real gap, worth raising at the next review.
within 10 both sides broadly agree.
−10 or lower they are harder on themselves than we are — often where an improvement plan is already working.
no gap shown only one side answered, or nobody rated — not a zero gap.

{{ gap_note }} Each side is averaged separately and a side with no rated response stays blank rather than counting as zero, so a KPI only one team answered cannot manufacture a gap. Response counts are shown next to each average because a 40-point gap built on one reply and a 40-point gap built on twelve are different conversations.
