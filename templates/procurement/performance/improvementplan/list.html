No improvement plans yet

A period that produced a critical band and no plan is the gap worth closing: the number is evidence, and the plan is what somebody agreed to do about it. Open one against the failing KPI, name what was observed and what “fixed” looks like, and give it a date — then activate it, monitor the result and close it with an outcome anyone can read afterwards.

Open a plan Clear filters

A plan runs draft → active → monitoring → closed, and can be cancelled from any of the three open states. Monitoring is kept apart from closed on purpose: a supplier that fixes a process in week two has proved nothing until the next period’s numbers come in. Only a closed plan carries an outcome, and only an admin can write one — closing signs the ending the supplier will be shown. “Past due” is measured against the extension when one was granted, never against the original target.
