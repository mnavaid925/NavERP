No exception matches this filter

Nothing in the queue answers to the search and filters above — which is not the same as nothing needing attention. Clear them with Reset to go back to the default view: open, acknowledged, and any snooze whose window has run out.

Nothing needs attention

Either nothing is breaching, or everything that was has been closed. Exceptions arrive here from two places: a KPI target with alerting switched on crossing its warning or critical band, and the rule-based detectors — dead stock, expiring lots, projected stockouts, demand spikes, shipments at risk or gone quiet, risky suppliers, expiring contracts, off-contract spend, price variance and freight variance. A workspace administrator can run detection now from the button above, and you can raise an exception by hand for something no rule covers. Closed alerts are not gone: switch the status filter to "All statuses" to read them back.

The default view is what still needs attention — open, acknowledged, and any snooze whose window has run out. A live snooze is deliberately hidden, because counting a suppression you asked for would make snoozing pointless; the count is on the tile above and the rows are one filter away. Impact is the value at risk, and it is what the queue is ranked by: acting on the biggest exception first is the whole point of an inbox that has more rows than hours.

Running detection is a workspace-administrator action — it is the only thing in Analytics that writes rows, and it is a batch that evaluates every alerting target plus each rule-based pass. A breach that keeps breaching re-fires the alert that is already open and moves its "last seen" stamp; it never adds a second row. Nothing here posts stock or writes to the ledger: an alert points at the rows that caused it and records what a person decided.
