There is no model artefact, no training set and nothing to retrain. Each score is a weighted blend of components measured directly from purchase orders, goods receipts, non-conformances, contracts, catalogs and the stock ledger — re-weighted across whichever components could be scored, so a supplier with no quality history is not silently given a zero for it. Every component's points, weight, contribution and underlying evidence are shown below, and the same figure is frozen into a snapshot so a past assessment stays reproducible.
