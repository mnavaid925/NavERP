{{ operational_estimate_note }}

Supply chain posts no journal entry, here or anywhere else. The ledger belongs to Accounting: its Balance Sheet and its Profit & Loss are the reportable figures, they are the ones built from posted journal entries inside closed fiscal periods, and they are what gets filed. Nothing on this page is a substitute for either of them, and no total below has been reconciled to one.

What this page has that the ledger cannot is the operational cut — which item, which customer, which lane, which leg of the cost stack. The ledger books a sale; it does not book the pick line, the freight bill and the return write-down behind that sale. That is the question this page answers, and it is an operating question, not a reporting one.
