How to read this report

Limits in force
On the excursion log these are the limits snapshotted when the episode opened, so the record still reads correctly next year after somebody legitimately re-bands the equipment. On the profile table they are the monitor's band today, because that is what the window was judged against.
Duration
The stored, minute-weighted figure once an episode has ended; the live elapsed time while it is still breaching. A row that says “still breaching” is counting now.
Excess
How far past the snapshotted limit the worst reading went. Blank when no extreme was recorded, or when the breached side has no limit — an excess of zero would mean it touched the limit exactly.
Reportable
The episode outlived its monitor's grace period. Derived against the setting as it stands now, never stored, so changing the grace period moves this answer.
% time in range
Minutes inside the band over the minutes measured, weighted by each reading's own logging interval. Blank when there were no readings, or when the monitor carries no band to judge against; 0 % is a real answer and means never once inside it.
MKT
Mean kinetic temperature, USP <1079.2>, weighted by each reading's interval. Deliberately absent for frozen, deep-frozen and cryogenic points — see the note above the table.
Calibration
In date · due inside the notice window · overdue · not recorded. The last of those is its own state and never green.
Dismissed
Judged not worth acting on, kept apart from “closed” so the log can tell “we fixed it” from “we decided it was a non-event”.

Every figure marked with an em dash is unanswerable, not zero. That distinction is the point of the report: a green 0 that actually means “we had no data” stops somebody going to look, while a blank sends them. The CSV download applies the same rule and writes n/a in place of every missing value.

Nothing on this page writes anything. Acknowledging, assessing, closing and dismissing all happen on an excursion's own detail page, one hop through the eye — and each of those actions writes one row to the application audit log with the user, the moment and the fields that changed.
