No reefer matches

A unit appears on this board once it is an asset with an active cold-chain monitor pointed at it — there is no “reefer” flag to tick, deliberately. Either nothing matches the filters above, or no monitor has been deployed against an asset yet. Clear the filters, check the asset register, or deploy a monitor onto the unit you want watched.

How each column is worked out

Reefer
An asset that has at least one cold-chain monitor in the active status pointed at it. Retire the monitor and the unit leaves this board on the next load — no flag to remember to clear.
Latest temperature
The newest reading on the unit's own probe, with the monitor's verdict beside it. A dash means the probe has not reported, which is not the same as a cold trailer.
Setpoint gap
Latest temperature minus what the unit was told to hold. Blank when there is no recorded setpoint or no reading; 0.00 means exactly on setpoint and still prints.
Breaching now
An episode with no end time — the cargo is outside its limits at this moment. Different from “awaiting triage”, which counts episodes that still need a person whether or not they have stopped.
Preventive programme
The worst due state across the unit's active plans, from Asset Management's own due arithmetic — calendar, meter or condition, whichever arrives first. “No PM plan” is a finding, not a compliant machine.
Open jobs
Work orders on this asset that are requested, approved, scheduled, in progress or on hold. Raised, worked and closed on Asset Management's own pages.
Meter
The newest reading on the asset's primary meter. Readings logged under another meter name are deliberately not substituted — running hours handed to a plan measured in kilometres would schedule a service roughly never.
Warranty
Asset Management's own warranty chip. “Not recorded” is its own muted state and never green.

There is no edit, no delete and no verb on this board. Every record it shows belongs to another part of the system — the asset, its plans, its jobs and its meter to Asset Management, the probe and its episodes to cold chain — and this page only assembles the view. Follow the links to act on any of them.

The whole board costs nine flat queries no matter how many units are on it: the active probes, the assets, the probes' newest readings, the running episodes, the outstanding triage counts, the plans, the plans' primed meter readings, the open jobs and the assets' own meter log. Not one of them runs per row.
