The timestamp is the end of the interval this row summarises and it is deliberately not defaulted to now: a defaulted "now" would file an imported logger row under the moment of the upload, turning a back-dated journey into a claim that the whole thing happened during the import. Back-dating is supported and expected; post-dating is refused, because a future row sorts to the top of an append-only log and becomes "the current value" for a store that has not reached it yet.
Temperature is signed — minus eighteen is the normal operating point of half of this module — and it is required, never defaulted. A blank temperature is not filed as 0 °C anywhere in this sub-module, on this form or in the importer: zero is a perfectly plausible temperature, so a substituted zero would read as a measurement somebody took, which is the worst thing that can happen to a record that ends up in an audit pack. Humidity is optional and most probes do not report it; leaving it blank records that fact rather than claiming bone-dry air. {% if monitor.deployed_on %}
This deployment started on {{ monitor.deployed_on|date:"M d, Y" }}, and a reading dated before then is refused — on a re-usable logger such a row belongs to an earlier deployment, and filing it here would attribute another consignment's journey to this one. {% endif %}

Optional — a single sample has no extremes

Leave both extremes blank for a reading you took once with a thermometer — that is a single sample and it genuinely has no minimum or maximum, which is a different statement from having one of 0 °C. If you do fill them in they have to bracket the representative value: the minimum cannot be warmer than the reading and the maximum cannot be colder. The two are checked independently, so a logger that reports only a maximum is perfectly legal — refusing it would push somebody into inventing a minimum.

{{ provenance_note }}

A reading filed here is stamped manual entry, attributed to your own party record, and weighted at the monitor's current logging interval. None of the three is on this form, and that is the mechanism rather than an omission — a source somebody can choose would let a hand-keyed row claim to have come from a sensor that never saw the goods.

One interval summary per moment per monitor. Re-typing a timestamp that already exists is refused with a message rather than silently doubling the series — the same rule that makes re-uploading a logger file a safe no-op. If a reading is wrong, file the correct one at a later timestamp: nothing here is ever edited, and the monitor always reads the newest row, so the correction is believed immediately.
