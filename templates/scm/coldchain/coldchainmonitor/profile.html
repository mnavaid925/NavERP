This project ships no charting library and no arithmetic filter that can offset a SIGNED temperature into a pixel offset — and half of 4.15 runs at -18 °C, so a naive height-as-percent bar would render every frozen reading as nothing. What IS computable here, exactly and without inventing a number, is the band verdict per interval, which the view already resolved into `out`. So this draws a BAND STRIP: one segment per interval, coloured by whether the goods were inside their limits, in time order.
