The last three filters are arithmetic rather than columns. Band state compares the newest reading against the limits, and unknown is not out of range — a point with no reading, or with no limit on either side, cannot be judged, and a page that painted it red would cry wolf until the red meant nothing. Device health asks whether the probe is still sending, where never reported is its own answer because "not yet commissioned" and "stopped reporting" are different problems with different fixes. Calibration reads the certificate against today, with {{ calibration_notice_days }} days of notice; "no certificate recorded" is kept apart from "in date" because it is a data gap and not a due date that has not arrived yet.
