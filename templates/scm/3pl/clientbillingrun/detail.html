A line is written only where there is something to charge for: a quantity inside the rate line's tier band, above whatever allowance the tariff includes. Flat recurring and dedicated-space lines are the exception and are always written, because a commitment is owed whether or not it was used — that is what a commitment is. Per-pallet-position storage bills stock units one for one as pallet positions until items carry dimensions, and each such line says so in its own description; a stated approximation is honest, a hidden one is not.

The ladder runs draft → calculated → approved → invoiced, and it only goes forwards. Calculate is available while the run is draft or calculated and may be pressed as often as you like; approving freezes the figures; drafting the invoice hands them over. Void is the way back out, and only before the invoice exists — once Accounts Receivable is holding a draft invoice for this period, the document to correct is that invoice and not this run.
