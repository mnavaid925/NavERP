Nothing in this workspace prices a 3PL client. Create one with New Rate Card: pick the client, give the card a name and a start date, then add the priced lines — storage per pallet position, a handling charge per order, a receipt fee, whatever the contract says. A card starts as a draft and only an active one is billed against, so nothing goes on an invoice until you activate it. If you were expecting rows here, try clearing the filters.

A rate card is evidence as much as configuration. Once a billing run has priced against it the card can no longer be deleted — the run snapshots each line's category, basis and rate onto its own rows precisely so the arithmetic stays readable years later, and the card is what those snapshots refer back to. Raise a new version and supersede the old one when prices change; the superseded card keeps its dates and its lines, and every past run keeps pointing at the tariff that was actually in force.
