Nothing on this screen writes to the stock ledger and nothing here holds a stock balance. A client owns stock because their items and their dedicated bins carry an owner — one nullable column on each, added additively with no backfill — and every quantity this sub-module reports is derived back through that link over the append-only ledger. There is deliberately no owner column on a stock movement itself: an append-only ledger with its own owner field would be a second source of truth, and the day it disagreed with the item there would be no way to tell which one was right.
