ClientRateCardLine carries NO tenant column — it is a pure child resolved through rate_card__tenant=request.tenant, and that join IS the authorization check — so a parent pk in the body is precisely how a crafted request would graft a priced line onto another workspace's tariff, with no column left to catch it. The parent's status is the VIEW's refusal, not a field error: both views redirect to the card with a message once it has left draft, because an illegal move must answer with a redirect and a field error cannot. So this page is only ever reached on an editable tariff. The tier band and the period rule are model clean() guards, restated below as help rather than duplicated as JS: `period` is REQUIRED on a periodic basis and REFUSED on any other, and `tier_to` must sit above `tier_from`.
