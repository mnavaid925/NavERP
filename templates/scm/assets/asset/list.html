Asset Register

Supply Chain›Assets

Every maintainable thing the plant owns — machine, vehicle, rack, facility — and its place in the plant. An asset's status is a human statement of intent and no work order ever changes it; whether a machine is down right now is derived instead, from the open downtime windows on its jobs. Reliability, cost and availability have no columns here at all: they are recomputed from the jobs and meter readings that produce them, which is why nothing on this page can quietly go stale.
