Where every figure comes from

On hand is the sum of every movement in 4.3's append-only StockMove ledger for that item. There is no stored quantity column anywhere in this application, which is precisely why this page cannot drift from the storeman's count — there is nothing to drift from. Value is that quantity at the item's cached average cost; the authoritative FIFO/LIFO layer walk is the valuation report's job and is not repeated here.

Min / max is the buyer's own ReorderRule: the min is the reorder point, the max is the level a suggested order brings stock back up to. The headline figure compares network-wide on-hand against the strictest configured point (falling back to the item's own default when no rule exists); the per-location chips beside it are the finer answer, each computed against that rule's own location. A part with 0 on hand and no policy configured is not flagged as below point — it is simply unstocked, and flagging it would drown the one signal this page exists to give. Shortages are actioned through reorder alerts and a purchase requisition, exactly as any other item.

Consumed counts movements of type maintenance inside the window above — the negative ledger entries a work order writes when it issues parts. That is the whole loop: a job issues a part, the ledger records it, this column counts it and the asset's repair spend picks it up on the depreciation report. Nothing on this page moves stock; issuing does, and it happens on the job.
