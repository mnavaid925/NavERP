Read at is when the gauge was looked at; logged is when it reached this system. The two differ whenever an observation is back-dated, which is supported and ordinary — a reading taken in the yard on Friday and typed up on Monday is still a Friday reading. Post-dating one is refused outright: a reading dated ahead would sort to the top of this log and become the current value for a machine that has not reached it yet, silently advancing every meter-based due date. Recorded by is stamped from the signed-in user's party when the reading is saved, never typed — who took a reading is an audit fact, not an input, and a blank here means the login was not tied to a party rather than that nobody took it.
