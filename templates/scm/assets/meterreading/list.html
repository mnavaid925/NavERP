Meter Readings

Supply Chain›Meter Readings

Every observation of every meter: the odometer at 41 200 km, the hour counter at 8 640 hours, the bearing temperature at 78 °C. A meter's current value is the newest row here, never a column somebody overwrites — which is what makes "how many hours did this machine run last month" answerable at all, and what lets a meter-based maintenance plan and a condition trigger compare a real observation against a target.
