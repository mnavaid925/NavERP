The meter name is free text on purpose: an asset commonly carries hours and an odometer and a temperature, and each is its own series. Keep the spelling consistent for a given meter — a reading filed as "Hours" and one filed as "Running hours" are two meters as far as this system, and every trend and due date built on them, is concerned. The reading is an assertion about the world, so it is bounded and rejected on the way in rather than quietly clamped: a silently corrected odometer would corrupt every due date derived from it while looking like a successful save.

Leave read at as it is for a reading taken just now, or set it back for one taken earlier — back-dating an observation is supported and ordinary. It cannot be set in the future: the save is refused, because a reading dated ahead would sort to the top of the log and become the current value for a machine that has not reached it yet, advancing every meter-based due date off a number nobody observed.

Provenance

A reading filed here is recorded as a manual observation with no source document, because that is what it is. Neither the source nor the reference is asked for: they are the reading's provenance, and they are stamped by whatever files the reading — a reading captured while completing a maintenance job is stamped by that job and carries its MWO- number, which is how the job's own readings panel finds it. Letting either be typed here would let a hand-entered figure claim to have come from work that never happened, on a log that is append-only and has no edit route to take it back.
