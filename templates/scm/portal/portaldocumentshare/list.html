Supply Chain›Document Shares

A share is a grant: one document, made retrievable by one portal account, until it lapses or somebody kills it. This is the only page in 4.16 that hands out a file rather than a projection of rows the customer already owns, so every line here is read access to another company's paperwork — and the two questions worth asking of it are can this still be fetched and was it ever opened. Both are answered below without ever printing the link itself.

State is a question about two nullable timestamps and the clock, not a stored column, and it must never become one: the answer changes by time passing, so a column would need something running every minute to keep it true — and it would go stale in the direction that says a dead link is live. A blank expiry means no expiry, which is what makes an old share worth hunting: a forwarded link outlives the person it was sent to. Revoked wins over an expiry that has also passed, because revocation is a deliberate act with a timestamp somebody may one day have to defend.

No document shares

Nothing in this workspace has been published to a customer, so no paperwork of ours is retrievable from outside it — which is the safe state, not a gap to fill. Publishing a document grants one portal account read access to one record until the expiry you set, and every retrieval after that is counted and dated on the row itself. If you were expecting rows here, try clearing the filters.
{% include "partials/pagination.html" %}
Revoke, do not delete. Revoking kills the link and keeps the three columns that make we published that proof of delivery on the 3rd and they downloaded it on the 4th defensible; deleting removes them with the row, which is why it sits on the same rung as revoking rather than a lower one. Neither this page nor the detail page prints the share link, and there is no copy-link button anywhere in the module: the token is a bearer credential nobody can rotate, and the customer reaches a live share through their own signed-in portal instead. Renewing a lapsed share is an edit to its expiry — the link itself never changes, so a customer who kept the old mail can use it again the moment the date moves.
