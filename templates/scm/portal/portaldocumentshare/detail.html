{% comment %} ============================================================ 1. the fact the whole page turns on Stated at the top rather than buried beside the revoke button: a person renewing an expiry or deciding whether to delete a row needs to know what a share actually IS before they touch either. {% endcomment %}

This link is a bearer credential

Whoever holds it can fetch this document

The URL behind this share carries an unguessable token and no login. Anyone holding it — the person we sent it to, anyone they forward it to, anyone reading a mailbox it lands in — can retrieve {% if pointer_label %}the {{ pointer_label|lower }}{% else %}this document{% endif %} until it expires or is revoked. That is what makes a share convenient, and it is why the two controls below exist: an expiry date is the only thing that ends this on its own, and revoking is the only thing that ends it now.

Four things are re-checked on every fetch, so this row is not the only guard: the token must match, this workspace must still be active, the share must be un-revoked and inside its expiry, and {{ obj.portal_account.customer }}'s portal account must still be active — deactivating the account kills its live links without touching a single share row. A refused fetch 404s rather than explaining itself: a revoked share is indistinguishable from a wrong token, so it leaks not even its own existence.

{% comment %} The absence of a copy-link button is deliberate and is explained ON the page, because an unexplained omission is the kind of thing a later change "fixes". See the header comment and the view's module docstring. {% endcomment %}

There is no copy-link button here, on purpose The token is never rendered on a staff page — not in a column, not a tooltip, not an attribute — because printing it once puts a value nobody can rotate into browser cache, screenshots, print-to-PDF and any proxy that logs response bodies. The staff queryset does not even load the column. The customer reaches a live share through their own signed-in portal documents page, which is the one place the download route is legitimately linked, so nothing is lost by leaving it off this screen. If a share has to be handed out again, the honest move is to publish a fresh one with an expiry — a new grant is visible in the register and leaves a create row; a quietly copied token leaves nothing.

Editing is admin-only here and nowhere else in the module, and the reason is easy to mistake for a convention: the expiry date is on that form, blank means never expires, and revoking is admin-gated — so a member who could edit could grant a share immortality on a link only an administrator may kill.
