Portal Accounts

Supply Chain›Portal Accounts

One row per customer: what their portal shows them, how much of the live stock figure they may read, and which documents and financials they are entitled to. This row is not a login. A customer is only actually on the portal when three separate things line up — an active CRM portal access exists for one of their users, that access names their party, and an active account here configures what the party may see. Each of the three is administered on a different screen and each can be true on its own while the customer still sees nothing, so the two columns worth reading first are Users and Last sign-in.

No portal accounts

No customer in this workspace has a portal. That is not a gap to fill in bulk — enablement is a deliberate per-customer opt-in, and until a row exists here a customer simply has no portal at all, whatever access their users hold in CRM. Nothing on this screen writes and nothing here is a promise. The notification preferences on each account — shipment, delivery, exception, preferred channel — are stored intent and this module dispatches nothing: no email, no SMS, no webhook, exactly as the order-confirmation stamps have sat unfired since 4.5. They are captured now so that on the day a mailer exists it reads a column carrying real customer intent instead of defaulting everyone to email. Prices and stock are the same posture from the other direction: the account configures how those figures are rendered and stores neither, so a change here takes effect on the customer's very next page load rather than the next time something is recalculated.
