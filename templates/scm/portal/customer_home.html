Every section below therefore carries its own .empty-state and the page reads as a working portal with nothing in it yet. None is rendered as an em-dash and never as 0: "no invoice exists" is not "you owe nothing", and "no shipment yet" is not "an ETA of zero". Counts that are genuinely counted (0 shipments on an order) say so in words instead of printing a bare 0.
