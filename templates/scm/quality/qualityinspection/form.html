Result rows are snapshotted from the inspection plan, never hand-added — there is no blank row here on purpose. Name a plan above and save, or use Generate results on the detail page.
The verdict on a measurement is derived from the snapshotted limits and overwrites whatever is selected here — record the measured value and the row judges itself. On a pass / fail or visual check the inspector's verdict stands, because a human read it and decided; an instruction has nothing to judge and is always not applicable. A half-filled sheet saves fine: "every mandatory row must carry a value" is enforced when the inspection is completed, not while the clipboard is still in someone's hand.
