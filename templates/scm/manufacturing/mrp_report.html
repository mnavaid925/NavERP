{% comment %} Two honesty notes the page must carry, both stated in the view's docstring: MRP emits suggestions rather than documents, and open purchase orders are excluded from supply on purpose. Written out here so a planner reading the numbers knows what they do and don't include, rather than discovering it from the source. {% endcomment %}

These rows are suggestions, not orders. A planner converts each one into a work order (make) or a purchase requisition (buy) — MRP never raises a document by itself, the same compute-then-convert contract the reorder alerts and the safety-stock calculator hold to. A silent auto-PO is a business decision nobody made.

Open purchase orders are not counted as incoming supply. Purchase-order lines carry no item foreign key — procurement uses free-text lines with a SKU hint — so matching them back to an item here would be a guess. The shortfall may therefore be already covered by stock on its way in; check open POs before converting a buy row.

Demand is open sales-order lines dated inside the horizon plus the outstanding components of released and in-progress runs. Anything with an active, effective BOM is exploded to its raw materials, so the requirement lands on what actually has to be bought or made. Required is netted against on-hand plus the item's active safety stock; only rows that come out short are listed.
