The rows together may not exceed what was approved on the line — receive less and approve more on the return if more came back than was authorised. Leave a row at received — awaiting decision unless the decision is already obvious; grading and deciding are separate acts, and the decision can be corrected right up until the moment the ledger sees it.

A lot or serial is required on a tracked item and must belong to the item on this line — returned serialised goods that land in the ledger with no lot are untraceable, and pairing one item with another's lot corrupts a ledger that cannot be corrected in place. A restock needs somewhere to go and it may not be the bench it is standing on, an expired lot may not be restocked at all, and a reason that blocks restocking refuses it outright — on this form and again inside the transaction that would write the ledger row.

Rows cannot be removed here. Once a row has posted to the ledger it is the only evidence of a movement that really happened; removing an unposted row is a delete on the row itself, from the bench.
