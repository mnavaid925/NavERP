Nothing on the bench

Receive an approved return line to open a bench row: record how many units came back, which lot, what grade they are in and which bench they landed on. Nothing is posted to the stock ledger at intake — the decision that follows is what moves stock, if anything does.
{% include "partials/pagination.html" %}

Intake posts nothing, deliberately. A warehouse location in this application is a warehouse, zone, bin, staging or transit — there is no blocked-stock type — and on-hand is the sum of the ledger across every location. Anything posted at intake would inflate on-hand, inventory value, the valuation report and the reorder and safety-stock inputs, tenant-wide and indistinguishable from sellable stock. Keeping the bench off-ledger is the blocked-stock stand-in, and it is what makes the posted flag on each row trustworthy. The honest cost: goods physically standing on the bench are absent from inventory value until they are dispositioned — the awaiting-disposition report is what covers that, not a ledger row.

A restocked unit re-enters stock at its graded cost, never at what the customer paid. For an item valued FIFO or LIFO the valuation report and the quick on-hand × average-cost figure will disagree afterwards — that is expected and is not a bug: the report walks real cost layers while the quick figure rolls a weighted average only part of the way down.

No credit-only return is outstanding. When one is, it appears here rather than being silently dropped by a bench that only knows about goods.
A credit-only return has no physical unit, so it never produces a bench row and its received quantity is permanently zero. Any queue keyed on "received" would quietly drop it — perfectly valid work that looks abandoned. It is named here instead, and it is settled from the refund queue.
