Supply Chain›Return Reasons

A reason is policy, not a label. What the customer says went wrong decides who pays the return freight, whether the unit may ever go back into sellable stock, and which outcomes they may even be offered. The reason is the input; the bench disposition is the output — the two are never collapsed into one field, so a returns clerk can always overrule the customer's story.
