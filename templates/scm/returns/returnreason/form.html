{% comment %} Every rule below is enforced by ReturnReasonForm.clean() and mirrored on the model, so the note a configurer reads and the validation that refuses their save cannot drift apart. The fee/fault sentence describes what the policy does with these fields, not a second rule. {% endcomment %}

The code is unique per workspace — a duplicate is caught on the form rather than thrown as a database error on save. A reason must allow at least one of refund, store credit, exchange or repair: one that offers nothing would be shown on the portal, picked by a shopper, and then refuse every outcome with no explanation.

Blocks restocking and suggested disposition cannot contradict each other. A reason that can never put the unit back into sellable stock may not also suggest restocking it — the save is refused. Blocking is enforced twice more downstream: the bench decide form drops restock and refurbish, and the ledger write re-checks it inside its own transaction.

Fault party is money, not commentary. It decides who pays the return freight when the policy says "whoever is at fault", and it is the signal for whether a supplier warranty claim is worth raising. Waives return fee suppresses the policy's restocking fee outright. Raises a non-conformance is what puts the "Raise NCR" button on a bench row against this reason — the quality link is data-driven, never a hard-coded list of reason codes.
