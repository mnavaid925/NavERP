Nothing has been refunded ahead of the goods. A return only appears here when it is flagged as an advance refund, and it needs a deadline — the deadline is the only thing that makes this page actionable rather than a list of worries.
{% include "partials/pagination.html" %}

At risk is the refunded amount on returns with units still outstanding; once everything has come back the exposure is zero even though the refund still happened. A row is overdue when its deadline has passed and the customer has never said they shipped anything — the same two conditions the deadline filter above uses, so the filtered list and the badged rows always agree.

There is no card hold and no payment gateway in this application, so an advance refund is a flag, a deadline and this report. Reversing one is a manual credit-note reversal in Accounts Receivable.
