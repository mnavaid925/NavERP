A claim needs at least one cost line — a claim with no costs asks the supplier for nothing. The lines are typed (part, labour, freight, external service, administration, other) because the normal real outcome is a partial approval that accepts the part and refuses the labour, and a flat claim value could never express that. Once the claim leaves draft the lines are frozen: they are what the supplier was shown, so nothing may be added, changed or removed and a fresh claim is raised instead.
