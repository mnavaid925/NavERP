Supply Chain›Return Authorizations

The customer's authorisation to send goods back. An RMA is a non-posting document: it moves no stock and posts no journal entry. Goods reach the ledger only when a bench row is dispositioned, and money only when a draft credit note is issued from Accounts Receivable.
