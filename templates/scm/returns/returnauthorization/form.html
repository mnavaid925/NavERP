{% comment %} Three money columns that look redundant and are not. `unit_price` is what the CUSTOMER PAID and sizes the credit; `unit_cost` is what the unit COST US and seeds the restock cost — a restock posted at the sale price would roll Item.average_cost upward and overstate the whole valuation report; `tax_pct` is the snapshot of the sold line's rate, and without it every refund silently under-credits the customer by the VAT. {% endcomment %}
A return needs at least one line. The same order line may not appear twice, and the quantity requested is capped at what was ever ordered on that line, counting the other live returns against it — that cap is deliberately weak, because nothing in this codebase records a shipped or delivered quantity per line; over-return is genuinely caught on the receiving bench. Leave Qty approved at zero and approving the return fills it in with the full requested quantity; a partial approval is an edit you make here, so that it is visible rather than buried in the approval. A photo is only required where the reason says so, and the approval refuses without it. Picking an order line narrows nothing automatically — check that the item, price and tax rate match the sale.
