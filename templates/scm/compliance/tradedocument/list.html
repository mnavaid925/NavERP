A trade document records what was declared on one piece of import/export paperwork — the parties, the transport as printed on the form, the declared value and the licence the movement is authorised under. Issuing one is what charges that licence's balance. If you were expecting rows here, try clearing the filters.
{% include "partials/pagination.html" %}
Ordered by issue date, newest first, then by most recently created — so a document that carries no issue date yet sorts to the end rather than jumping the queue. Status is never typed: it moves only through the issue / submit / accept / void verbs on each document's own page, because issuing is what spends a licence balance and voiding is what gives it back. A document keeps two numbers on purpose — ours (TD-) is how we find it, the external one is what the broker, the bank and the customs officer quote back. The declared value shown here is what the filer declared, not a sum of the lines; whether the two agree is on each document's own page, where the arithmetic costs one query instead of one per row.
