What movement it papers

All five are optional link-outs to rows the transport, procurement and order sub-modules own — this document re-declares none of their addresses, tracking events or proof of delivery. The licence is the one that matters most: issuing this document charges that licence's balance, and issuing is refused outright if the balance will not cover it. A draft licence can be named here on purpose, because paperwork is prepared against a licence long before that licence goes active.

Who the parties are

All three dropdowns offer the whole party book rather than one role. An export consignee is a customer, an import shipper is a supplier, and the notify party is routinely a broker or forwarder — narrowing to any single role would hide the majority of legitimate counterparties on the majority of documents.

The money on the form

The declared value is the one money figure that is stored rather than computed: it is what the filer declared, not a total of the lines below. The document's own page shows when the two diverge by more than a cent, which is a customs problem rather than a rounding one. The Incoterm list is Incoterms 2020 — a term outside it is refused, because an Incoterm is a legal allocation of cost and risk and an invented one means nothing to the counterparty reading the form.

What is in the box

Net weight is the goods alone and gross weight includes the packaging, so a net larger than the gross is refused on save — the reverse is not a smaller number, it is a packing list that gets rejected at the border.

Leave a row blank to skip it; tick Remove to delete an existing line. There is deliberately no "at least one line" rule — an ocean bill of lading, an insurance certificate and a licence copy legitimately carry none, and the declared value above is what those documents state. The description, HS code, origin and printed unit are snapshots: typed here once and never back-filled from the item master, because a filed declaration has to keep saying what was declared even after the item is re-classified. The line value is not a column — it is quantity × unit value, computed on read, so it can never be edited into disagreeing with its own two factors.
