Method & limitations

For each shipment: tonne-km = (weight_kg ÷ 1000) × load distance_km, then gCO2e = tonne-km × the factor for its mode (falling back to its load's mode). Totals are the sum over every measurable shipment; the intensity above is the blended realised g/tonne-km, i.e. total grams ÷ total tonne-km, not an average of averages.
