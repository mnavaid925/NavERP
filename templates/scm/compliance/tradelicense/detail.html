The two used figures are the only stored derived numbers on this record, and they are a cache with a stated invalidation path: every writer of a charging document re-derives them, and the Recompute action below re-derives them again from the documents themselves, so drift can never become permanent. Everything computed from them — remaining, utilisation — is recomputed on read, because a stored "remaining" would be a second copy of the same fact that could disagree with the first. Only issued, submitted and accepted documents count; a draft or a voided one spends nothing, which is why {{ document_count }} document{{ document_count|pluralize }} can sit under a licence that only {{ charging_count }} of them charge.
