Ordered soonest due first — an obligation with no date at all sorts to the top, because an unscheduled duty needs a human more than a dated one does. Opening this page also rolls the stored status forward for the rows that actually crossed a due date, in one write: a status is a column but overdue is a fact about a date, and the two have to catch up somewhere. Only applicable, in progress and overdue rows are ever moved that way — compliant, non-compliant, not-applicable and retired are human decisions and a clock must not walk them back. The pass rate is not in this table on purpose: it is derived from each row's own checks, so a column of it would cost one aggregate per row. It is on the requirement's page.
