One dated ESG scorecard per trading partner — the four themes that vote on the medal, Carbon on its own separate scorecard, the declarations a compliance team actually asks for (REACH, RoHS, conflict minerals, forced labour, deforestation, code of conduct) and the supplier's own Scope 1/2/3 figures. This is not 4.2's risk assessment: risk asks what a supplier could do to us, this asks how a supplier behaves. Both live on each row's page, side by side, so neither can be read as the other.

Newest assessment date first. The medal is the mean of the themes that were actually scored — an unanswered theme is a question nobody asked, not a zero, so a partial scorecard is normal and is not punished for its gaps. Carbon does not vote: it is scored, stored and shown on its own separate scorecard exactly as EcoVadis does it, so a strong carbon page cannot lift a supplier over a weak labour theme. The Scope 1/2/3 figures behind the carbon footprint report are declared by the supplier; the freight emissions on that page are the ones this system works out itself from 4.6's shipments, and the two are never added together.
