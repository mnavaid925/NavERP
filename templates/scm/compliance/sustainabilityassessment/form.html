Scorecard

Each theme is 0–100, and every one of them is optional. Only the themes you fill in are averaged into the overall score, so leaving one blank costs the supplier nothing — entering 0 is a measured failing grade and is a completely different statement. Carbon is scored separately and does not vote on the medal: it is shown next to it, exactly as EcoVadis does it, so a strong carbon page cannot lift a supplier over a weak labour theme. Score nothing at all and the assessment is saved with no medal and no maturity level rather than a zero.

Emissions declared by the supplier

Enter these only as the supplier reported them, in tonnes CO2e — this system never computes them and never verifies them, and the carbon report keeps them visibly apart from the freight emissions it works out itself from shipment weights and distances. Leave a scope blank when it was not reported: blank and 0 are stored and shown as different answers, and a blank scope is simply left out of the declared total rather than counted as nothing emitted. The reporting year is what the carbon report groups by, so it is bounded 2000–2100.
