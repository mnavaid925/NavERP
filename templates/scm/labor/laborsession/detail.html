Accuracy
{% if accuracy_pct is None %}—{% else %}{{ accuracy_pct|floatformat:2 }}%{% endif %}
Clean units as a percentage of units completed, derived from the recorded error count and never typed. An em dash means no units were completed at all — a shift of pure indirect time has no accuracy, and 0% would put somebody who spent the day in training at the bottom of a quality ranking.

Every activity's measurement is a snapshot taken when it was filed — the fixed time, the per-unit rate and the allowance are copied onto the row, so re-timing a standard next month cannot retroactively turn this shift's passing performance into a failing one. Deleting a standard clears the pointer and leaves every measurement it ever produced intact.
