Labor Sessions

Supply Chain›Labor Sessions

One worker's shift at one facility, and the minutes it is accountable for. A session says a person was on site between two clock times; the activities booked underneath it say what those minutes went on — direct work, indirect work, lost time. This is the shift execution record and deliberately not a second attendance table: HR still owns daily attendance, and the two reconcile on worker and work date rather than through a foreign key.
