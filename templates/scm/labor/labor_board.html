How this board is worked out

Three queries, one per task table, each fetching at most {{ max_bulk_assign }} of the oldest open rows with its progress figures already annotated onto them. "Open" is each model's own definition — a pick that is still editable or still pickable, a put-away in its open statuses, a cycle count that is scheduled or in progress — so a status added to any of them appears here automatically rather than quietly dropping out of the queue. Age is the only ordering the merged board uses, because the row that has been waiting longest is the one somebody has to act on.

Stale is a display band and nothing else: past {{ stale_after_hours }} hours a row is chipped, and no refusal, expiry or escalation hangs off it. It is a fixed threshold rather than "older than average" on purpose — a threshold derived from the set it is grading moves with the data and stops being a threshold.

There is no edit and no delete on a row here. These tasks belong to 4.4 Warehouse Management, which owns their lifecycle, their lines and their stock consequences; this page holds a single column of theirs and a read-only view of the rest. The eye opens the real record, one hop away, and every other change is made there.

Assignment re-fetches every ticked id inside the workspace and inside the kind's own open statuses before writing, so a foreign id and a task that has moved on since the page loaded are skipped rather than forced, and each genuine change writes one narrow update and one audit entry. Assigning the same person twice writes nothing and audits nothing — the second press is a no-op, not a duplicate trail.
