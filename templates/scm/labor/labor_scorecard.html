Ranked workers first, then the ones that have a performance figure, then that figure descending, then name. Every hour figure is to two decimals; a dash means the question has no answer for that worker in this window, and is never the same thing as 0.00 — which is why 0.00 is still printed wherever it is the true answer.

How each column is worked out

Rank
Position among workers who cleared {{ min_ranked_minutes }} booked minutes and have a performance figure. A dash is "not in the ordering", never last place.
Band
Not measured · below sample size · coaching band · at or above standard. Chosen by the report, from the performance figure and the sample size, and stored nowhere.
Work days
Distinct work dates with a scored shift. Two shifts on one day is one work day.
Attended
Clock-out minus clock-in, summed. Blank while no shift of theirs in the window has been clocked out.
Booked
Every minute booked to an activity on those shifts, direct and indirect together.
Direct
Value-adding work — picking, packing, put-away, counting, loading.
Indirect
Necessary time that moves no unit — travel, breaks, training, meetings, waiting, cleaning.
Units
Quantity handled on direct activities. Indirect time moves no unit and contributes none.
Units / hr
Units ÷ all direct hours. A throughput fact about every direct minute worked, whether or not a standard existed for it.
Performance
Earned ÷ the direct time that carried a standard. Unmeasured minutes are excluded from BOTH sides, so work nobody has time-studied yet cannot drag somebody down.
Accuracy
(Units − error units) ÷ units, on direct activities. Blank when no unit was handled — there is no accuracy over no work.
Utilisation
Booked ÷ attended, over closed shifts only. A shift with no clock-out yet has no denominator, so it is left out rather than divided by nothing.

Every one of these is unanswerable rather than zero when its denominator is missing, and prints as a dash. That is not a display nicety: 0% performance against a named person is a statement about how they spent their week, and this page will not make one it cannot support. The distinction survives all the way into the band — "Not measured" and "Coaching band" are different findings, and only one of them is about the worker.

The standard behind every earned minute is a snapshot taken when the activity was booked, not a live lookup — so re-timing a job, or archiving the standard entirely, never retrospectively re-scores work that was already measured under the old rule. Change what a job is expected to take in the standards library; it applies from the moment it goes live, forwards.
