Labor Activities

Supply Chain›Labor Activities

One row per stretch of a shift: what the worker was doing, for how long, how many units came out of it and how many of those were wrong. A shift says a person was on site between two clock times; these rows say what those minutes went on — and every productivity figure in the module is an aggregate over this table.

Shifts Standards Scorecard

Minutes are booked on the shift, not here

Read across every shift

This register is a workspace-wide read across every shift — it is where a supervisor filters, compares and finds a booking, and it is deliberately not a second place to file one. An activity is minutes inside one shift's clock window, so it is booked from that shift's own page: open the shift and press Book activity. The parent then comes from the address of the page rather than from a field anybody could type, which is what stops minutes being grafted onto somebody else's shift.

A booking can only be added, corrected or removed while its shift is still open. Closing a shift freezes its activities, and approving one is the payroll export's lock — which is why the Edit and Delete actions below disappear on a signed-off shift rather than failing when pressed.

Ordered by start time, newest first. Earned is what the labor standard in force at the time says this quantity should have taken, and performance is that figure over the minutes actually booked — 100% is working exactly to standard and above it is faster. Both are blank, never zero, on work no standard covers: not every job in a warehouse has been time-studied, and printing 0% against a named person would render an unmeasured job as a failing one. Both are computed from a snapshot taken when the row was filed, so re-timing a standard next month cannot restate last month's figures. Nothing on this page writes a stock movement or a journal entry — booking minutes against a 4.4 pick, put-away or count task sets a pointer out to it and adds nothing to it.
