This is the only figure on the line a human may write, and the note beside it is where the reason goes — a training day, a leaver not yet replaced, a cushion held before a peak. Overstaffing is reported and never refused: a deliberate margin is a legitimate plan, and the number exists so it stays a decision somebody made rather than one nobody saw. Fractional values are accepted for the same reason the required figure is fractional — half a person on put-away is half a person, and rounding it at the line would hide how the bodies are actually shared.
