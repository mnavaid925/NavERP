Labor Standards

Supply Chain›Labor Standards

A standard is how long one unit of one activity is expected to take, in one scope, over one date range — and it is the keystone of the whole sub-module: without one, a labor module is a timeclock. Every performance figure downstream is earned minutes over actual minutes, and earned minutes come from exactly one formula: (setup + travel + qty × per-unit) × (1 + allowance%). Nothing on this page measures anything on its own — only an active standard whose window covers today is ever selected.

No labor standards

Nothing in this workspace says how long any activity should take, so every minute booked on the floor is recorded as unmeasured — no earned minutes and no performance figure anywhere, which is the honest answer rather than a zero. Write a standard, activate it, and from that moment work in its scope is measured against it. If you were expecting rows here, try clearing the filters.

Nothing on this screen writes, and nothing here re-derives a minute: earned minutes exist in one method, and every page in 4.14 routes through it rather than keeping a second copy of the formula. A standard only starts measuring when somebody presses Activate on its own page, because activating redefines what every future booking earns and archiving silently removes a scope's measuring stick — both belong on a button with the consequence printed beside it, not on a dropdown somebody scrolls past while fixing a typo. Editing a live standard is allowed and cannot rewrite history: each booked activity snapshots the determinants it was measured under, so a re-time changes what future work earns and nothing else.
