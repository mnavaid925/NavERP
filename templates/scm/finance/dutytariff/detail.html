Why this is not an accounting tax code. Accounting's tax codes cover sales tax, VAT, GST and use tax — their vocabulary has no customs member, they carry no HS code and no origin, and they are not effective-dated against a transaction date. A duty needs all three. So the customs rate lives here and the sales-tax rate stays there; this register does not re-declare a sales tax, and the pointer above is how the two meet.

Rates supersede rather than overwrite. The right way to change a duty is to close this window and add a new row starting the day after — not to edit the percentage in place. Editing rewrites what the book says was true last month, and the whole reason a tariff is effective-dated is so a landed cost worked out in January can still be explained in June.
