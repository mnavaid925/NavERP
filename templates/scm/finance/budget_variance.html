Supply Chain› Finance & Accounting Integration› Budgeting

The accounting budget for each department beside what supply chain has committed to spend and what it has already incurred. Committed money is ordered but not yet spent — it is the part a budget report built from invoices alone never sees, and the part that turns an overspend into something you can still act on.

No budget selected

There is nothing to compare against. Supply chain does not hold its own budget — this page reads accounting’s budget lines and groups them by the department on each line. Create a budget with lines that carry an org unit, and the departments will appear here beside what has been requisitioned, ordered and spent against them. If a budget already exists, check that its lines have a department set: lines with none land on the Unassigned row rather than a department of their own.
