{% if can_allocate %} Press Allocate below and every capitalising charge is spread across the inbound stock movements this receipt posted, on the basis each charge asks for. Each item's average cost is rolled once, by the total that landed on it — not once per row — and the last row of every charge absorbs the rounding remainder, so the amounts spread add up to the charge exactly. {% else %} This voucher cannot be allocated in its current state. A cancelled voucher and one that has already been billed are both closed to further allocation: the cost on the items is the cost that was agreed, and a page that re-spread it afterwards would leave the ledger and the item master disagreeing. {% endif %}

Only the cached average cost moves — no quantity is ever written, and no stock movement is created, amended or reversed by allocating. The stock ledger is append-only and stays exactly as the receipt left it. If a basis cannot be measured on every row — no weights on the items, say — the arithmetic falls back to quantity and then to an equal split, and the basis it actually used is stamped on every row above rather than left to be inferred.

The ladder runs draft → allocated → accrued → reconciled. Allocate may be pressed again as often as the charges change — it always backs the previous roll out before putting the new one on, so pressing it twice does not cost the goods twice. Cancel is the way back out, and only before a bill exists: once Accounts Payable is holding a draft bill for these charges, the document to correct is that bill and not this voucher. Edit and Delete disappear as soon as the voucher is allocated, for the same reason — the cost is inside real item valuations by then.
