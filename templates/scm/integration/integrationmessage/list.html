No add · No edit · No delete

Rows below carry a View action and, where the row is not already pending or acknowledged, a Reprocess button. There is no Add, no Edit and no Delete, and none of the three is missing: an exchange log is evidence of what crossed the boundary, and a row somebody can rewrite after a partner has acted on it is not evidence of anything. To correct a mistaken exchange, the corrected document is appended as a new row — the same posture the stock ledger takes with a compensating movement and the general ledger takes with a reversal.

Reprocess re-queues a row for handling: it sets the status back to Pending, bumps the attempt count and clears the recorded error. It does not alter what the row says arrived, and it sends nothing — 4.19 ships no outbound transport, so nothing is called, signed or delivered by pressing it. Pressing it opens the row so you can see the result.

Rows arrive here from the exchange itself and from nowhere else — no screen in this application writes one by hand, and none edits or removes one. If a row looks wrong, that is what the log says happened; the corrected document is appended alongside it and the pair is read together. Staff changes to endpoints and subscriptions are a different trail entirely and live in the audit log, unchanged. Payload excerpts are truncated by design and may contain partner PII — a ship-to name and address on an 856 is ordinary — so a full document body is never stored, here or anywhere in 4.19.
