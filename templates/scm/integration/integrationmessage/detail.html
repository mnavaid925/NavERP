        There is no Edit and no Delete on this page, and neither is missing. An exchange log is evidence of what crossed the boundary; a row somebody could revise after the other party acted on it would not be evidence, and one they could remove would be worse. A mistaken exchange is corrected by appending the corrected document as a new row, and the pair is read together — the same posture the stock ledger takes with a compensating movement.
