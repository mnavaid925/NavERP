No credential on this page is usable, by design. What is stored for each connection is a short prefix plus a one-way hash — enough to say a credential is registered and which one it is, and not enough to authenticate with. That is the right trade only because 4.19 ships no transport: nothing signs and nothing calls a provider, so there is nothing to lose by keeping a marker instead of a secret. The day real outbound calls are built, the fix is encryption at rest with the key held outside the row — never a plaintext column, and never a hash that no longer works because a hash cannot be used to sign or to log in.
