Webhook Subscriptions

Supply Chain›Webhook Subscriptions

A subscription is a standing rule: when this happens to one of our documents, tell that URL about it. Pick the entity to watch, the event that matters, and where the payload goes. Each subscription keeps its own signing secret so the receiver can prove a call really came from us — and its own delivery log, so a partner insisting they were never notified can be answered with a record rather than a recollection.

No webhook subscriptions

Nothing is set up to notify anyone when a document changes here, so a partner who needs to know that a shipment was delivered or a goods receipt was posted has to come and look. Add a subscription for each event somebody downstream depends on: choose the entity and the event, give it the URL to call, and generate a signing secret so the receiver can prove the call is ours.
