What this page does not do

NavERP records commercial terms somebody wrote down. It does not enforce them.

Nothing in NavERP consults this catalog, or a plan entitlement, at request time. A grant is a record, not a gate: writing a row here grants a customer nothing by itself, and no feature is switched on or off as a result. Entitlement enforcement is declined by this sub-module; the code column exists so a future consumer would have a stable key to join on, and there is no such consumer today.

These rows are also deliberately not the same thing as core.FeatureFlag, which is a runtime on/off switch. A price-list entry and a kill switch are two different facts, and merging them would mean switching a feature off rewrote what past subscriptions were entitled to.
