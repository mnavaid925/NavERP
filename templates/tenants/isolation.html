Every model carries a tenant foreign key and every view filters Model.objects.filter(tenant=request.tenant) — never .all(). There is no per-tenant database or schema: isolation is enforced by that scoping discipline, which is why the cross-tenant checks live in each module's own security test lane rather than in one central place.

What this page cannot prove

It reports the posture, not a guarantee. Cross-tenant access control is exercised by the per-module security test lanes (a cross-tenant detail request must 404), not by this page — a green badge here means the tenant FK exists on the model, not that every view filters on it. Row-level encryption at rest, a KMS/HSM-backed key store, SIEM forwarding and per-tenant physical isolation are not implemented; they belong to 0.7, 0.16 and 0.17.
