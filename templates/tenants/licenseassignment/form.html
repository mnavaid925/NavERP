Leave the module blank for a tenant-wide seat. A seat is identified by this workspace, this person and this module, so the same person cannot hold two seats for one module here. A source is vocabulary an operator records by hand — nothing in NavERP assigns a seat from a group or a rule, so direct is the only value the system writes itself.

A subscription is the billing link, not part of seat identity. Leaving it empty is fine; deleting a subscription later will not delete anybody’s seat history.

NavERP records commercial terms somebody wrote down. It does not enforce them. Seat auto-deprovisioning is declined — there is no identity sync, so reclaiming a seat does not disable the login of the person who held it. And a seat recorded here grants no access by itself: nothing consults it at request time. It is a register entry saying who holds what.
