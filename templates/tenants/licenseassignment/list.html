What this page does not do

NavERP records commercial terms somebody wrote down. It does not enforce them.

Seat auto-deprovisioning is declined. There is no identity sync in NavERP, so reclaiming a seat does not disable the login of the person who held it — it records a commercial fact and nothing more. Their account, their password and their access to every other module are untouched. If you need a person removed, that is an accounts task, not a seat task.

A seat here does not grant access either: it is a register entry saying who holds what, not a permission that any code consults. A blank module is a tenant-wide seat, a real value rather than “any module” chosen implicitly.

Expired in the Derived column is computed from expires_on, not a stored status — nothing in NavERP writes status="expired", so a row can show Active and still be past its end date. The two columns are separate on purpose.

The source column is vocabulary somebody records by hand. Nothing in NavERP assigns a seat from a group or a rule; direct is the only value the system itself ever writes.

A reader landing here sees a total, a plan entitlement page shows a number per plan, and the subscription form has a plain seat field. Left unstated, the obvious assumption is that they are the same number enforced three times. They are not, and deliberately so: 0.19 records terms, it does not reconcile them. Saying so is cheaper than an operator filing a bug about a mismatch that is the designed state. {% endcomment %}

Three seat numbers, deliberately not reconciled

A seat count appears in three places, and NavERP does not make them agree:

  • This register — the rows above. The record of who actually holds a seat.
  • The seats entitlement — what the plan and any subscription override grant (plan grants).
  • Subscription.seats — a purchased figure on the subscription itself.

Nothing enforces one against the others, and a subscription override of 75 over a plan allowance of 50 is a legitimate record rather than a conflict. Reconciling the three is deferred; if you need a single answer, this register is the one that answers “who actually holds one”.
