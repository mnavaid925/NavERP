Subscriptions

NavERP records commercial terms somebody wrote down. It does not enforce them.

Automatic renewal execution is declined. auto_renew is a recorded intent with no scheduler behind it — there is no cron, no worker and no queue in tenants, so nothing renews anything. What this board does not do

Automatic renewal execution is declined. auto_renew is a recorded intent with no scheduler behind it. There is no cron, no worker and no queue in this app, so nothing renews anything: no card is charged, no new term is written, no subscription is extended. A subscription flagged for auto-renew reaches its date and is simply past it, exactly like one that was never flagged. A scheduler is a later sub-module’s job (0.20), not this one.

Expiry email delivery is declined. There is no mail sender in tenants, so no email is sent when a subscription expires — not a warning to the customer, not a notice to your team, and not a renewal receipt. The expiry exists only as a date on this page. Nothing is scheduled, queued or dispatched anywhere.

A grace window is a date somebody recorded. It does not extend anything, hold access open, or prevent a subscription from reading as past its date — the In grace marker is a label on a row, not a behaviour.

L36 â€” which rows are 0.1’s. This board is new in 0.19, but the rows underneath it are not: Subscription and SubscriptionInvoice were built by sub-module 0.1 and are extended here, never re-declared — this sub-module added two columns to the subscription, nothing more. If you are looking for a second billing table, there is none; reporting a duplicate-billing bug here would file it against the wrong sub-module.

Prepaid credit grants and rate-card, tiered or multi-currency pricing are declined app-wide, and proration is declined — no money arithmetic is performed anywhere in this sub-module, so no figure on this page is a computed price.
