What this page does not do

NavERP records commercial terms somebody wrote down. It does not enforce them.

Quota enforcement and throttling are declined. This row is a ceiling on paper. Nothing throttles a user, computes an overage charge, or blocks a request because of it, and the policy on breach is a recorded intent with no interceptor behind it.

Consumption is 0.1’s usage register, joined on this same subscription — the quota board is where the two are compared. There is no event pipeline: usage rows are entered by hand or by 0.1’s flow, so the meter only moves when somebody records a row.

A limit of 0 means unmetered, not “zero permitted”.
