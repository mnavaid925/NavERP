What this page does not do

NavERP records commercial terms somebody wrote down. It does not enforce them.

Quota enforcement and throttling are declined. action_on_breach — alert, charge or block — is a recorded policy with no interceptor. Marking a quota breached writes a timestamp and an audit row, and nothing else happens: no alert is raised, no charge is computed, no request is blocked, nothing is throttled. The verdict is a note an operator left.

A limit of 0 means unmetered, not “zero allowed” — the same distinction UsageRecord.included_allowance draws by returning None. Unmetered rows carry no percentage and no breach state.

These rows are the commercial ceiling only. This is not an anti-abuse control: the abuse bound belongs to core.IpAccessRule, and operational thresholds belong to core.AlertRule. The consumption these ceilings are measured against is 0.1’s usage rows, joined on the same subscription so a quota can never be measured against a different subscription’s usage.
