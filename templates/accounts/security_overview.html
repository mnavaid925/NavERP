What this module does NOT do

No SAML 2.0 or OAuth/OIDC federation (Azure AD, Okta, Google Workspace) — that needs an identity-provider library and a configured IdP. No SMS or email OTP, no push approval, no FIDO2/WebAuthn passkeys. No breach-corpus password check and no passwordless sign-in. No geo-IP or behavioural risk analysis — the score uses only the IP, device and recent-failure signals this app can observe. No SIEM forwarding (that is 0.9). Declared here rather than implied.
