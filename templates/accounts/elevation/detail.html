What this grant does and does not do

It records and time-boxes a privileged window. It does not change the user's is_tenant_admin flag — that stays an explicit admin action, because the repo has no scheduler and a privilege that grants itself on a timer would never come back off. There is also no credential vaulting (no secret is stored here) and no session recording (nothing captures a session). Both are declared rather than implied.
