Security overview

The policy applies only when "Apply this policy" is on — that is deliberate. Turning these rules on for every workspace the moment the feature shipped would have invalidated live credentials nobody asked to change.

Complexity and rotation are implemented. Breach detection is not (it needs a breach corpus such as HIBP and an outbound call this repo does not make) and passwordless is not (no WebAuthn).
