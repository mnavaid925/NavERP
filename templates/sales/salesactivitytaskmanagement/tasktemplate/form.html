Both directions are enforced, because "every account in a territory" is unreachable without the territory, and "every open opportunity" plus a territory is ambiguous about which of the two narrows the set. Disabled means Django reads the field from the instance, never the POST, so a crafted request cannot attach one anyway. * `cadence_interval` locks at 1 for a One-Off (G6) — "one-off" and "every seven" are two valid fields describing a contradiction. * The task-type and priority lists are IMPORTED from CRM 1.5's CrmTask, never re-spelled here. * `last_generated_on` / `generated_count` are `editable=False` FROZEN RECEIPT.
