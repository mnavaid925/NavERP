Where these numbers come from

Open, click and reply counters sit on the sales activity row as frozen evidence. They are not on any form in this module: they move only through three audited POST actions on an activity's own page, and they only ever move up — a later click never un-opens an email, so "was it ever opened" cannot be un-answered.
