What the server decides, printed rather than implied: * The email counters (opened / clicked / replied) are `editable=False` FROZEN EVIDENCE, so Django excludes them from this form without being asked. A rep is precisely the one party with an incentive to type "opened 47 times", which is why a typed counter would be worthless. * `capture_source` and `is_auto_captured` must agree (G3). The checkbox is pre-written from the chosen source on POST, and the model refuses the pair if they ever disagree. * `occurred_on` and `planned_minutes` are stamped once, by the model, when the row is first saved.
