An ActivityType does NOT own the interaction. `crm.CommunicationLog` (CRM 1.5) records the call, the email and the meeting; this catalogue says which KIND of activity it was and what defaults a rep should not retype. `kind` and `default_outcome` are the CRM 1.5 vocabularies IMPORTED, not copied (L36) — there is no second channel list anywhere in 8.8. ENUM filter selection is reflected from the ECHO keys the view passes (`kind`, `active`); every control rendered here is a control the view actually APPLIES — they are declared in `crud_list`'s `filters` spec, not merely handed to the template (L54's third rule). Only badge-green/amber/info/muted/red/slate exist in theme.css; badge-success/warning/danger do NOT and render unstyled (L33). Every badge below keys on the EXACT choice value with an {% else %} get__display fallback. Delete is a POST form with a csrf token and a confirm naming {{ obj.number }} — the SYSTEM-assigned ATY document number, never free text an author typed.
