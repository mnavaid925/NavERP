{% extends "base.html" %} {% comment %} Impact report — FROZEN evidence on the left, LIVE figures on the right. This is the page the entity exists for, and the whole design is one idea: an approver is signing off a NUMBER, so the number must be frozen, while the order it will be applied to keeps moving. The two columns are labelled in PROSE, not just in a heading, because the difference between them is the finding: * FROZEN = what the amendment looked like when it was proposed or last edited. Server- generated, never authorable, never recomputed on read (L22). This is what gets approved. * LIVE = the order as it stands right now, plus the reservations and shipments that exist right now. This is what the change would actually land on. When they agree, the approval was taken against the current state. When they do not, somebody edited the order in SCM, or made an allocation, or raised a shipment since. A page showing two totals with no indication which is the evidence and which is the rumour is how an approval goes wrong. Read-only. Nothing here mutates and nothing here re-freezes the snapshot. {% endcomment %} {% block title %}Impact · {{ amendment.number }}{% endblock %} {% block content %}
