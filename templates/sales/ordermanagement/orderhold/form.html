{% comment %} Printed rather than implied, because every one of these is a field the form does NOT offer — and a user who cannot see why is a user who asks for them to be added. {% endcomment %}
FieldWhy it is not on this form
SeverityCopied from the rule that fired. A hold's severity is a fact about a past decision, not a preference — re-tuning a rule next month must not rewrite why an order was held today.
StatusWorkflow. Only the raise, clear and supersede verbs move a hold between open, cleared and superseded.
Evaluation snapshotFrozen evidence, written by the server at the moment the hold is raised: the figure observed, the threshold it breached, the order total and who raised it. A snapshot typed by hand would be an account of the decision written by the person the decision was made about.
Checkout & timestampsAction-stamped. Each is written by exactly one named button, and the checkout is exclusive — two people cannot work the same hold.
