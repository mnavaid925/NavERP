How the three figures relate

{% comment %} The same explanation as on the schedule form, at the level of one obligation. A user who cannot see WHY there is no amount field will ask for one. {% endcomment %}
FigureWhere it comes from
AllocatedThis obligation's share of the contract value, worked out in Python by recompute() and rounded to cents. Not a field, and not editable.
RecognisedWritten by recompute() once the recognition date has passed, oldest obligation first, spending a shared budget of the contract value.
RemainingAllocated less recognised, read live. It is what "overdue" is measured against — a passed date with nothing left to recognise is history, not lateness.
