{% extends "base.html" %} {% comment %} Create / edit one RevenueSchedule. One template serves both: `is_edit` and `obj` are pinned on BOTH create and edit (obj is None on create), so the Back link and the Cancel target are the only two things that differ. The six fields below are the WHOLE authorable surface of a schedule, and one of them — `status` — is the single authorable status in 8.6. A schedule is drafted, activated and voided by a person, not by a workflow verb, so putting the picker here is the point: going ACTIVE is the act that authorises revenue recognition, and `recompute()` refuses on anything else. There is no money field here and there cannot be one. The contract value, the allocation, the recognised figure and the deferred balance are all @property values worked out from the order and the obligations. They are not columns, so they are not on this form, and the second table below says so for every figure a user is likely to go looking for. {% endcomment %} {% block title %}{% if is_edit %}Edit Revenue Schedule{% else %}Open Revenue Schedule{% endif %}{% endblock %} {% block content %}

A revenue schedule states, under ASC 606, which part of a customer order has been earned and which part is still being held. The order itself belongs to SCM — this record points at it, reads its total, and writes nothing on it.

Figure or fieldWhy it is not on this form
Contract valueDerived from the order's total, read live from SCM 4.5. It is not a column here, so it cannot be typed — and it moves by itself the moment an amendment re-prices the order. A typed copy would be a snapshot of another module's arithmetic that silently disagreed with it.
Allocated amountWorked out in Python by recompute() from each obligation's share of the contract value. editable=False, off every form, and written by that one method only.
Recognised amountAlso written only by recompute(), and only as far as the passed recognition dates allow. A person who could type it would be asserting the answer the schedule exists to work out.
Deferred / contract asset / contract liabilityAll three are arithmetic on the two above. None is stored, so there is nothing to save and nothing that can fall out of date.
Journal entryReference-only and editable=False. 8.6 posts NOTHING (L29) — it states the revenue position and the ledger is another module's job. A form carrying this would let a clerk record that revenue was posted when nothing was posted.
Performance obligationsAdded on the schedule's own page, one at a time, through the parent. An obligation that reached its schedule from a form field would be a way to attach a promise to somebody else's contract.
NumberSystem-assigned RVS- at save time, and it is what every confirm dialog and audit row names.
