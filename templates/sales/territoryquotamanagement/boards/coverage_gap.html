{% extends "base.html" %} {% comment %} 8.7 Coverage gap board — every way coverage can be incomplete, SURFACED rather than hidden. Six independent ways an account or a territory can end up uncovered, and they are deliberately six separate tables rather than one merged list, because they have different owners and different fixes: * uncovered — no active rule matched the account * unassigned — no live assignment row at all * over-assigned — more than one live primary, or auxiliary alignments over the cap * manager-less — an active crm.Territory with manager IS NULL * orphans — a live assignment whose territory was deleted (the SET_NULL case) * orphaned pairs — a live SDR membership pairing with no active account executive The orphan tables exist because deleting a CRM territory orphans its assignment rows rather than cascading them away. That is the right call for a record of a decision, and it has an obligation attached: the orphans have to be visible somewhere, and this is somewhere. No over-assignment

No account carries more than one live primary, and none is over the cap on live secondary and overlay alignments.

An account may hold one live PRIMARY and a bounded number of SECONDARY and OVERLAY alignments — that is the hunter/farmer split and the overlay specialist working alongside a base rep. What this table catches is going past that: two live primaries leave an account with no single owner.

Every live assignment points at a territory that still exists.

These rows are the SET_NULL orphans a deleted CRM territory leaves behind.
