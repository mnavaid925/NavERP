ONE template for both, driven by `is_edit`. `assigned_by` is `editable=False` frozen evidence: who made the assignment is a fact about a decision, not a field a later form edit may rewrite. That is why the create and edit views are hand-written rather than delegated to `crud_create` / `crud_edit` — those know how to stamp a tenant and an audit row, and nothing about a fact about itself. There is deliberately no input for it on this form. `assignment_source` and `rule` are bound to each other in the model: a rule-sourced row MUST name the rule that made it and every other source FORBIDS one. A row that claimed a rule it did not come from would make the audit trail lie about how an account was assigned, which is the one thing this ledger exists to say. {% endcomment %} {% block title %}{% if is_edit %}Edit Territory Assignment{% else %}New Territory Assignment{% endif %}{% endblock %} {% block content %}

FieldWhy it is not on this form
Number (TAS-)System-assigned and sequential. A hand-typed number is how two books of coverage history end up sharing a document identity.
Assigned byFrozen evidence, stamped once from the signed-in user at the moment the row is written. A form cannot carry a fact about itself, and a later edit must not be able to rewrite who made the call.
TerritoryOwned by CRM 1.2. Sales reads crm.Territory through a FK and declares no second territory master. A deleted territory orphans its rows on purpose, so this register can show the gap instead of hiding it behind a cascade.
