{% extends "base.html" %} {% comment %} 8.7 Account→territory assignments — the effective-dated LEDGER, not a pointer. Every row carries its own window, so an annual rebalance supersedes the previous assignment instead of overwriting it, and "which territory was this account in last March" stays answerable after the territories have been re-cut. `effective_to IS NULL` is the live question; there is no stored "is current" flag to drift. A row with no territory is the SET_NULL ORPHAN a deleted CRM territory leaves behind. It is surfaced here and on the coverage-gap board, not hidden behind a CASCADE — it is the record of a decision about a book of coverage. ENUM filters read their ECHO keys; FK dropdown compares are `|stringformat:"d"` against `request.GET.territory` / `request.GET.owner` — never `|slugify` on a pk (contract §11.1 rule 3). {% endcomment %} {% block title %}Account Territory Assignments{% endblock %} {% block content %}
