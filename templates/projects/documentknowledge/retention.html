The Run reminders button raises one in-app notification per in-window record into the project inbox. It is a button, not a schedule: this repository has no scheduler and no mail worker, so nothing is sent by email and nothing runs while nobody is looking. Pressing it twice changes nothing — the reminder's title carries the document number and its date, so an unchanged window cannot raise a second time while the reminder remains unread in the inbox.

How retention actually works here

{% comment %} The honest scope of bullet 5. Retention in this sub-module is a documented INTENT plus audited verbs: a window a human reads, a computed due date, an archive Toggle and a legal hold that BLOCKS. Automatic destruction, an immutable vault and proof-of-deletion artefacts are Module 13.9/13.14's, and no page here may claim otherwise. {% endcomment %}
The window
retention_months on the document feeds a computed retention date. Nothing is stored, so nothing goes stale.
The due date
Read against today's date on every page load. There is no nightly job to fall behind and no flag to forget to clear.
Archiving
A document leaves the live register and stays readable through the archive lens. It is the honest alternative to deleting evidence, and it is refused while a legal hold is in place.
Legal hold
While held, a record may not be archived and may not be deleted, and no new revision may be approved onto it. That is the whole guarantee.
What deletes
Nothing here, ever. Automatic destruction, retention enforcement, an immutable vault and proof-of-deletion artefacts are Module 13.9 / 13.14. This board owns the human-readable queue.
