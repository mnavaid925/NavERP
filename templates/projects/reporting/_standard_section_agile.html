Agile

Rows are sprints, placed in the month their start date falls in. The completed column counts cycles that closed with a completed status, and the count beside it is every sprint that started in that month — so an open sprint is already in the denominator. Committed points stay on the sprint itself; this register never adds them up, which is why throughput here is counted in cycles rather than estimated in points.
