Three readings here are only explainable from the compute, so this is the one place on the page that explains them: `hours` sums the row's own figure (`ResourceAllocation.total_hours` is null unless the booking used that unit), `billable_pct` has no source field on a booking at all, and `utilization_pct` is pinned to None in `_aggregate` because capacity lives on the resource record. {% endcomment %}

Resource

Hours are each row's own booked or logged figure, summed. A booking taken as hours per week or as a share of capacity records no total, so it adds nothing and the group reads 0 hours — the unit the booker chose, not a missing timesheet. The billable share is real only where hours were logged: a booking carries no billable flag, so a register of allocations shows 0% whatever its hours say, and utilisation against capacity is the resource board's arithmetic, not this register's.
