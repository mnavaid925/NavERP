Trend

The trend area answers across time, so it mixes a live count with ratios. Open counts rows in the window that have not closed yet; on-time is scored only on work that has actually landed. CPI and SPI each need both sides of a comparison — planned value to divide by, or actual cost to divide into — so a group with nothing booked reads as a dash, and a dash here means no answer rather than a poor one.
