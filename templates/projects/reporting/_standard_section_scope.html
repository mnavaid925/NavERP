Scope

Change orders are grouped by the status the scope module holds, and the exposure column is their cost impact summed — a request nobody has priced contributes a row but no money, so an unpriced change is missing from that total rather than free. Approved and implemented count as done; a cancelled request leaves the open count but stays in the register.
