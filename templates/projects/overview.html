RegisterWhat it isActions
Project Requests Demand intake, business case and the go/no-go decision.
Charter Register Scope, objectives, success criteria and executive sponsor.
Stakeholders The RACI matrix and the influence/interest engagement plan.
Kickoffs The launch ceremony and the baseline acknowledgement.
Task Register Every WBS node as a flat, filterable register.
Dependencies Task sequencing — link types, lag and lead between work packages.
Milestones Decision points and phase-gate criteria on every timeline.
Baselines Frozen schedules and what-if scenarios, with the active baseline flagged.
Resource Pool Bookable people — staff and contractors — with roles, teams and capacity.
Allocations Named bookings and placeholder roles, soft/firm, per project or pipeline request.
Time Entries Daily project hours with the approval queue and the actuals-to-plan comparison.
Capacity & Demand Over-allocation alerts and the unfilled demand that is the hiring trigger.
Budget Revisions Budget versions and change control — the approved-and-activated one is the baseline.
Budget Register Every budgeted line by category, revision, WBS package and control account.
Control Accounts The EVM panels — BAC, EV, CPI/SPI and the EAC forecast per deliverable.
Expenses Commitments, actuals and accruals; only posted rows count against the budget.
Risk Register Threats and opportunities with probability × impact, EMV and the response strategy.
Risk Analysis The probability/impact matrix, the EMV table and a seeded Monte Carlo run.
Response Actions Who owes which mitigation, by when, at what cost, and what activates it.
Issue Log Capture, severity, resolution and the current escalation level.
Escalations The recorded escalation path — level, target role, reason and outcome.
Risk Monitoring Top risks, the exposure burn-down, the review queue and the lessons lens.
