Stage a rollout

A rollout stage here is a declaration, not a control. Nothing selects a cohort, and 0.10's FeatureFlag.is_enabled is never written by this page. The stage and percentage say what a rollout was planned to reach, so a change record can be read back honestly.

A stage is a child of the change that owns it, which is why it has no number of its own: there is no ROLL- to look up, only the change request. The ladder is exact — internal only means 0%, general availability means 100%, and partial sits between them — because a contradictory declaration is not a record of anything.
