What this module does NOT do

Nothing sends. There is no outbound worker, no queue and no scheduler, so a rule routes and a provider records where a message would go; the sending itself happens in the module that owns the message. No per-tenant branding layer for messages — a template body is the whole message, and no logo or footer is injected from tenants.BrandingSetting. No automatic translation: locale is a stored column and a manual per-locale row, not negotiation from the recipient. No digest scheduler: the digest choice is recorded and nothing batches on it. No open/click tracking and no retry. Declared here rather than implied.
