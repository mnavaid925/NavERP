What this module does NOT do

No automatic personal-data collation across modules, so a DSAR access or portability request is driven to completion but the bundle is assembled by hand. No destruction of any kind — the retention board identifies and the disposal register evidences, and the act is out of band. No data-residency enforcement (the region is recorded only). No consent-enforcement middleware: recording a withdrawal does not by itself stop a module from mailing someone. No encryption at rest (that is 0.7). Declared here rather than implied.
