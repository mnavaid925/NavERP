Enterprise risks, scored on a five-by-five likelihood-and-impact scale and given a recorded treatment. This is not projects.ProjectRisk (module 9), which is a delivery risk on one project; the two are separate registers and neither rolls up into the other.

The score is calculated, never typed. inherent_score is recomputed from the likelihood and impact you pick, so the number on every row is the product of the two scales rather than a figure somebody asserted. Nothing in this application measures your risk appetite or warns you when a review is overdue.
