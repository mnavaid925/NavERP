This form records a change; it does not schedule, build, deploy or roll back one. Submitting it asks for a decision. Approving it records that a decision was made. Neither touches a running system, because this repository has no pipeline.

What is deliberately not on this form: requestor, approved_by, requested_at, approved_at, implemented_at, rollback_reason and rollback_at. The first two are stamped from the acting user by the Submit and Approve actions, so a change can never be raised or approved in somebody else's name — and an approval is precisely the act an audit exists to attribute. The rest are evidence stamps written by the action that performs the transition.

Risk and impact are two questions, not one severity: risk is how likely this is to go wrong, impact is what it does to users if it does. This vocabulary is deliberately NOT the alert severity list — a firing severity and an assessed change risk are different facts that happen to share three words.

Leave status at Draft — it is the only value on this dropdown. Submitting, approving and rolling back are separate actions on the change's own page, and each one stamps the requestor, the approver or the reason itself. A status typed here could claim a decision without the evidence behind it, so the control does not offer one.
