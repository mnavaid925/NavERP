A jurisdiction's statutory and e-invoicing obligation. This is deliberately separate from the tax rate: accounting.TaxCode owns how much tax applies, this owns how the document must be transmitted (Italy SDI, India GST IRN, PEPPOL). A rule can point at a tax code, or stand alone as a pure reporting obligation. Nothing here transmits an invoice — the obligation is recorded, not executed.
