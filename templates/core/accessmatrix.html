How to read this

Scope row means a policy is recorded. Enforcing means that policy would actually narrow what a member sees — it requires the scope to be enabled AND the data scope to be something other than "All records". A row that is configured but not enforcing is a recorded decision, not a restriction. Enforcement happens where a module's list view opts in; it is not retrofitted across the ~150 existing sub-modules, so a module with no opt-in shows here as configured-but-inert even at a narrower scope.
