What this board does NOT do

It reports what other apps recorded. There is no throughput or latency measurement — the integration tables carry no duration column, so “throughput” here is a count of rows, not a rate. No retry or dead-letter handling is performed: the failover and retry order elsewhere in this module is a recorded intention and nothing acts on it. No alerting on an erroring integration. Declared here rather than implied.
