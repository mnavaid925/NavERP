This form records a declaration; it does not schedule anything. NavERP has no scheduler, no queue and no worker pool. A cadence, a handler path, a pool and a failure policy entered here are an intention for a person to review — nothing in this application acts on any of them.

What is deliberately not on this form: last_run_at and next_run_at are system-stamped by a scheduler that does not exist. Letting a person type them would let the register assert a cadence it has never observed.
