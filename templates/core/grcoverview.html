Not built yet

Two of this sub-module's five bullets are deferred to a second pass, and they are named here rather than quietly omitted:

  • Audit & Certification Support — engagement records, evidence collection, auditor access and control attestation. The evidence collectors every vendor sells require a runtime that does not exist here, so nothing is stubbed to look present.
  • Data Residency & Sovereignty — region-pinned storage and jurisdiction-specific controls. NavERP is a single-region application with one database; it cannot pin storage, so a page claiming it would be the exact false claim the rest of this sub-module refuses.

The obligations of the privacy regimes themselves — DSAR clocks, consent, retention — are already built in core 0.8 and are not repeated here.
