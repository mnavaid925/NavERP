What this module does NOT do

No workflow engine. The repo already contains ~71 approval models across six apps and each decides its own routing; a platform engine would be a second source of truth for every one of them. This module owns the registry, the limits, the rules and the monitoring — not the decisions. No drag-and-drop designer: steps are recorded as an ordered register, not a canvas. Nothing executes: rules recommend, SLA rules record intent, and no escalation is sent. No auto-approve or auto-reject — there is no scheduler. Bottleneck analysis is counts and oldest-item age only, with no per-approver timing. Declared here rather than implied.
