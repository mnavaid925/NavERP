A register of what was backed up, archived, restored and rehearsed. Every figure below is counted from records a person wrote.

This is not a backup engine. NavERP has no scheduler, no object-storage client, and no ability to dump or restore its own database. Nothing on these pages takes a backup, restores one, replicates data or provisions an environment — the act happens out of band, and these rows are the trail it leaves.

Registers and boards

SurfaceWhat it holdsOpen
Backup jobs One row per backup a human reports as taken — type, target, encryption and whether anyone checked it.
Restores Restores as operations with a source, a target time and an outcome — never a button.
Archives The catalogue entry that records where archived data is — the loop that made a restore impossible.
Legal holds Preservation orders that suspend every retention window covering their scope, until released.
Environments Dev, test, staging and sandbox instances, their copy scope and their reaping date.
Recovery drills Rehearsals carrying measured RPO and RTO, judged against the posture's targets.
Recovery posture This workspace's DR targets — a singleton, edited rather than created.
Recovery board Every unverified backup, locationless archive and active hold, named rather than counted.
