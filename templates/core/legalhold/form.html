A hold suspends the retention schedule covering its scope. While it is in force, data that a policy would otherwise dispose of must be preserved. Set the scope carefully: everything within it stops being disposable.

A hold has no expiry. Its end is the release event, not a duration — so Released at is the field that ends it.
