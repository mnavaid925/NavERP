What this board does NOT do

It reports what other modules recorded. There is no open-rate or click tracking, no bounce handling beyond the statuses the owning modules already set, and no retry policy: the failover order above is a recorded intention and nothing acts on it. The window is by created_at, so a source without one cannot be time-bounded and is counted in full. Declared here rather than implied.
