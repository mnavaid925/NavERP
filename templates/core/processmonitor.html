What this board does NOT do

It reads; it does not act. No escalation is sent, nothing is auto-approved or auto-rejected, and no SLA rule fires — the repo has no scheduler, so those are recorded intentions and this board is where an overdue item becomes visible. Bottleneck analysis is limited to counts and oldest-item age: there is no per-approver timing, no cycle-time trend and no throughput history, because the approval tables do not carry the decision timestamps that would need.
