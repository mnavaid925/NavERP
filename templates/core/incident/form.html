This writes a notice; it does not publish one. NavERP has no status page, no subscriber list and no mailer. Filling this in does not tell anybody anything.

{% comment %} `notified_at` and `resolved_at` are deliberately absent. notified_at is written only by the POST-only "Record publication" action, and resolved_at is derived in the MODEL's save() from the status - so a typed resolution date would be an invented event. `started_at`, `scheduled_for` and `scheduled_until` ARE here: they are the schedule a person is declaring, not a stamp a system took, and a maintenance notice that cannot say when its window opens is not a notice. TenantModelForm already supplies the datetime-local widget and matching input_formats for all three. {% endcomment %}

Leaving progress blank is honest. A blank means nobody is tracking a percentage; 0 would claim a fix that is genuinely at zero.
