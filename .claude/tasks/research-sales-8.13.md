## 2. Ownership ruling (L29 / L36 / L37) — what this sub-module must NOT declare

The spine already owns most of what 8.13 needs. 8.13 **extends by FK**; it does not re-declare a single one of these.

| Need | Existing owner | How 8.13 uses it |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing campaign master (budget, actual spend, target size, UTM tags) | `crm.Campaign` (`CAM-`, CRM 1.3 Marketing Automation) | FK `campaign` on every 8.13 model |
| Lead master (status, score, source, owner, est. value) | `crm.Lead` (`LEAD-`, CRM 1.1) | FK `lead` on touchpoint / handoff / engagement |
| Deal master (stage, amount, probability, source campaign) | `crm.Opportunity` (`OPP-`, CRM 1.2) | FK `opportunity` on touchpoint / handoff |
| Customer account | `core.Party` + `PartyRole` role `customer` | FK `account` |
| Sales collateral / content master (title, type, document, tags, author) | `sales.EnablementContent` (`ECN-`, Sales 8.9) | FK `content` on `ContentEngagement` |
| Owner / rep / attendee | `accounts.User` | FK `owner`, `assigned_to`, `computed_by` |
| Currency | `accounting.Currency` | FK `currency` |

**Declared by 8.13 (new, own tables):** the touch ledger, the credit assignment, the MQL handoff, the content engagement event, and the marketing event (+ its registration child). Nothing else.

**No JournalEntry is posted by 8.13** (L29). Campaign ROI, pipeline and revenue figures are *marketing-side attribution* of revenue already recognised elsewhere; 8.13 never posts to the financial ledger. `crm.Campaign.actual_revenue` and `budget_actual` remain the campaign's own figures.

| **Zoho Webinar** | [zoho.com – Webinar for marketers](https://www.zoho.com/webinar/use-cases/marketers.html) | Attendee attribution and campaign ROI; confirmation/reminder/follow-up/thank-you email automation; attendee → lead → opportunity stitching |
| **InEvent** (event management) | [inevent.com – Event management features](https://inevent.com/blog/events/event-management-software-features.html) | UTM tracking baked into registration; every registration source tracked *in the same system as the attendee record*, not a separate analytics platform |

**Cross-product consensus (what every surveyed product does):**
1. Credit is assigned to **touchpoints**, not to campaigns — campaigns are the rollup.
2. A **touch ledger** records channel, interaction type, date and cost per interaction.
3. **Rule-based models** (first/last/linear/time-decay/U-shaped) are the default; **custom** credit is the escape hatch.
4. Attribution is only as good as **identity stitching** (one lead/contact across touches) and **UTM capture** — both of which NavERP gets free by FK'ing `crm.Lead` and `crm.Campaign` (which already carries `utm_source/utm_medium/utm_campaign`).
5. MQL→SQL is a **lifecycle handoff with a response SLA**, reported as an acceptance rate plus a **time-in-stage** figure.
6. Engagement is scored from **observed behaviour** (attendance, watch time, polls, clicks, downloads), and the score drives **prioritised follow-up**.
7. Event ROI is reported as **pipeline/revenue per registrant against cost per registrant**.

## 5. Recommended build scope (1–4 model budget)

The skill's guidance is 1–4 models per pass. 8.13 has **five** NavERP feature bullets, and every one of them needs its own navigable list page, so a strict 4-model cut would silently drop a bullet. The recommended scope is **5 entity folders / 6 model classes** (one is a child of its parent, per the entity-file rule) plus **1 derived console** and **no new master table**:

| Entity file | Models | Number prefix | NavERP bullet |
|---|---|---|---|
| `AttributionTouchpoints.py` | `AttributionTouchpoint` | `ATB` | Campaign Influence & Attribution |
| `CampaignAttributions.py` | `CampaignAttribution` | `ATR` | Campaign Influence & Attribution |
| `MqlHandoffs.py` | `MqlHandoff` | `MQL` | MQL-to-SQL Tracking |
| `ContentEngagements.py` | `ContentEngagement` | `CNG` | Content Performance & Engagement |
| `MarketingEvents.py` | `MarketingEvent`, `EventRegistration` (child) | `EVT` / — | Event & Webinar Management |
| *(derived console)* | — | — | Campaign Performance Integration |

**Prefix collision check** — every `NUMBER_PREFIX` already used in `apps/sales` was enumerated (`CMP, ACPL, QBR, CHS, COB, CRN, CADJ, CER, CMPL, CST, LNE, WLR, OUT, PIPE, OTM, AMD, OHD, OVR, RVS, CPQ, BND, QAR, SAL, ATY, SPC, TTPL, CSC, ECN, SCTF, SPB, FAD, FCP, FSC, FCS, TAS, QPA, TMB, TRG`). `ATB`, `ATR`, `MQL`, `CNG` and `EVT` are all free.

`EventRegistration` is a membership/attendance row, not a numbered document, so it carries its own tenant FK + timestamps and no number (same ruling as `crm.CampaignMember`).

## 6. Explicitly out of scope (carried to the gap file)

1. **W-shaped / full-path attribution** — needs lead-creation and opportunity-creation touch markers, which 8.13 does not model. (GAP)
2. **Data-driven (Markov / Shapley) attribution** — needs a conversion corpus at 2,000+ conversions/month; not viable in the demo dataset. (GAP)
3. **Ad-platform spend connectors** (Google Ads / LinkedIn / Meta) — no connector framework exists in NavERP; spend is entered on `crm.Campaign.budget_actual`. (GAP)
4. **Automated handoff generation** from a lead-score threshold — 8.1 `LeadScoreEvent` exists, but there is no rule engine to fire the handoff. (GAP)
5. **Automated confirmation / reminder / thank-you email sequences** for events — no marketing send worker exists; the seeder notes CRM email sends remain simulated. (GAP)
6. **Live webinar micro-engagement capture** (polls, Q&A, watch time) — needs a provider integration. (GAP)
7. **AI-recommended content per prospect** — Module 23 `ai` is not built. (GAP)
